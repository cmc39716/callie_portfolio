01 / The Project

Creative Identity of Glory Glory.

Glory Glory is a fan-first sports media organization dedicated to delivering exclusive content, unparalleled access, and unique experiences that strengthen the connection between University of Georgia athletics and its fans. From recruiting updates, behind-the-scenes coverage, mic'd up sessions, and exclusive podcasts to VIP experiences and lifestyle merchandise, Glory Glory creates opportunities for fans to engage with the athletes, traditions, and culture that define the University of Georgia.

Through a leadership role in photography, videography, and graphic design, the creative work focuses on developing engaging visual content that supports Glory Glory's mission. Combining composition, movement, typography, and authentic emotion, the work captures the energy of Georgia athletics while building a recognizable visual identity across digital platforms. From game-day photography and video production to social media graphics and exclusive athlete content, each deliverable contributes to a cohesive storytelling experience that brings fans closer to the programs and personalities they support.

02 / Creative Approach

Finding the story within the game.

Each shoot focuses on looking beyond traditional action photography and identifying moments that reveal the athletes' personalities. Framing, perspective, and timing help balance athletic movement with genuine emotion.

03 / Composition

Directing the viewer's attention.

Contrast, cropping, and composition establish clear focal points within each photograph. These decisions emphasize the athletes while preserving the details that communicate the atmosphere of the game.

04 / Photography & Motion

Extending the story through motion.

Video introduces movement, pacing, and sound as additional storytelling elements. Combining still photography with motion communicates individual moments alongside a broader sense of the athletes' experiences.

Every game has a story. Some of its best moments happen between the plays.

VISUAL STORYTELLING / 01

Connecting individual moments.

Rather than treating each photograph as a standalone image, the visuals are considered as part of a larger collection. Image selection and sequencing create a rhythm between action, anticipation, and emotion.

05 / GRAPHIC DESIGN INTEGRATION

Beyond the photograph.

Photography captures the moment, while graphic design gives that moment a new purpose. By integrating typography, color, composition, and image treatments, individual photographs become engaging visual content designed for digital platforms.

Each design balances the energy of Georgia athletics with a consistent visual direction that supports Glory Glory's identity.

THE CREATIVE PROCESS

Creating a visual language beyond game day.

The design process begins with selecting photography that communicates a clear message. Typography, scale, layering, and graphic elements are then introduced to establish hierarchy and direct the viewer's attention.

Rather than treating photography and graphic design as separate disciplines, the two work together to create a cohesive visual experience. This approach allows content to remain recognizable across different formats while adapting to the purpose of each deliverable.

06 / Final Deliverables

A visual identity built through storytelling.

The final collection brings together photography, graphic design, and video to create a consistent visual representation of Glory Glory Georgia. By balancing action-driven imagery with more personal moments, the work highlights athletes as individuals while communicating the collective identity of Georgia athletics.

07 / Reflection

Designing beyond the image.

This work demonstrates photography as a form of visual communication rather than simply documentation. Composition, image selection, and sequencing guide audiences through a story while maintaining a cohesive visual direction across platforms.

Intentional creative decisions support both the athletes being documented and the larger mission of connecting Georgia fans with the programs and personalities they support.
