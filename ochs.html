Photography / Visual Storytelling / Graphic Design

OCHS Soccer.

More than a game. A collection of moments that tell the story beyond the final whistle.

Project

Oconee County High School Soccer

Category

Sports Media & Visual Storytelling

Creative Focus

Photography Graphic Design Video Production Visual Storytelling
01 / The Project

More than the final score.

OCHS Soccer was a sports photography and visual storytelling project focused on documenting the team's identity beyond the action on the field. Through photography, video, and graphic design, I explored how composition, movement, and emotion could communicate the energy of the program.

The goal was to develop a cohesive collection of visuals that captured both the competitive atmosphere of each match and the personal moments that make the team unique.

02 / Creative Approach

Finding the story within the game.

I focused on moments that revealed the team's personality, using framing, perspective, and timing to balance athletic movement with genuine emotion.

03 / Composition

Directing the viewer's attention.

Contrast, cropping, and visual hierarchy helped establish clear focal points. Each composition was designed to emphasize the athletes while preserving the atmosphere and intensity of the match.

04 / Photography & Motion

Extending the story through motion.

The senior video introduced pacing, movement, and sound to the project. Combining still photography with video allowed me to connect individual moments to a broader story about the athletes.

Every match has a story. Some of its best moments happen between the plays.

05 / Visual Narrative

Connecting individual moments.

I selected and sequenced imagery to create a visual rhythm between action, anticipation, and emotion. This helped the photographs function as a cohesive story rather than isolated images.

06 / Final Deliverables

A consistent visual language for the team.

The final collection brings together photography, graphic design, and video. Each medium contributes to a recognizable visual identity while highlighting the athletes, traditions, and energy of OCHS Soccer.

07 / Reflection

Designing beyond the image.

This project strengthened my ability to approach photography as visual communication rather than simply documentation. I developed a deeper understanding of how composition, image selection, and sequencing guide an audience through a story.

I also learned to make more intentional creative decisions that support both the individual subject and the larger identity of the team.
