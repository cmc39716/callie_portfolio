01 / Overview

Redesigning the Story.

Missing in America is a true crime podcast series connected to Dateline NBC that investigates missing persons cases and shares the stories of individuals and families affected by their disappearances.

The podcast combines investigative journalism with personal storytelling, creating a serious and emotionally engaging listening experience.

The goal of this project was to redesign the existing podcast identity to better reflect its subject matter while improving legibility, hierarchy, and recognition across different digital platforms.

02 / The Problem

When the Message Gets Lost.

The original identity relied on a heavily centered composition, dark photographic imagery, and textured typography. These elements made the artwork difficult to recognize and interpret at smaller scales.

The background image was too dark and blurred to communicate a clear visual message. Rather than reflecting investigative journalism, the imagery suggested a fictional horror story or supernatural mystery.

The typography also presented problems. A textured letterform created a visual gap in the word "Missing," while similarly weighted typefaces weakened the hierarchy between the podcast title and supporting information.

The challenge was not simply to make the podcast look different, but to make its message clearer.

03 / Design Goals

Clarity Through Design.

The redesign needed to communicate the serious, real-world nature of the stories being told.

Rather than relying on dark, ambiguous imagery, I wanted to develop a recognizable visual symbol that could communicate mystery, investigation, and the search for answers.

Typography, composition, and color would work together to establish a clearer hierarchy and create an identity that remained legible across a range of applications.

01 / LEGIBILITY

Readable at Every Scale.

Develop typography and imagery that remain recognizable on podcast covers, mobile devices, and smaller screens.

02 / HIERARCHY

A Clear Visual Structure.

Move beyond a fully centered layout and create stronger relationships between the title, symbol, and supporting information.

03 / MEANING

Purposeful Imagery.

Replace ambiguous imagery with symbolism that connects more directly to investigation, discovery, and missing persons.

04 / Exploration

Searching for Direction.

I began the redesign by exploring different approaches to the podcast title, experimenting with letterforms, composition, and visual symbols.

Early sketches focused on the relationship between the words "Missing" and "America," testing different ways to create emphasis while maintaining readability.

This stage allowed me to compare a wide range of ideas before committing to a final direction.

05 / Typography

Building a Stronger Wordmark.

Typography was one of the primary weaknesses of the original identity. The textured lettering reduced legibility and created an unclear visual hierarchy.

I explored different weights, arrangements, and proportions to develop a wordmark that felt more direct and recognizable.

Simplifying the letterforms helped the title communicate more clearly, allowing the visual symbol to carry the expressive qualities of the identity.

06 / Visual Language

A Symbol of the Search.

The visual direction developed around a key-inspired graphic, using its recognizable form as the foundation for the identity.

The key creates a visual connection to mystery, discovery, and the search for answers without relying on literal photographs or horror-inspired imagery.

Its bold silhouette allows the symbol to remain recognizable at different sizes, while the distressed texture introduces a sense of age, uncertainty, and complexity.

07 / Color & Texture

Creating the Atmosphere.

The redesigned identity uses a restrained palette of muted blue-gray, deep burgundy, and black to create a serious, investigative atmosphere.

The blue-gray background provides contrast with the darker symbol, while the burgundy introduces tension and visual emphasis.

Texture adds depth to the composition without interfering with the readability of the podcast title.

08 / Final Identity

A More Recognizable Identity.

The final identity combines a bold key-inspired symbol, simplified typography, and a restrained color palette.

The composition creates a stronger relationship between image and type while giving each element a distinct role within the visual hierarchy.

Rather than using an unclear background photograph, the redesigned artwork relies on recognizable graphic forms to establish its visual message.

The Final
Design.

A more focused visual identity designed for recognition, readability, and digital use.

09 / Applications

Designed for Digital.

Podcast artwork needs to work across a wide range of digital platforms, from large promotional graphics to small thumbnails within streaming applications.

I applied the redesigned identity to mobile listening interfaces and smartwatch mockups to demonstrate how the artwork translates across different screen sizes.

These applications demonstrate the importance of a recognizable symbol, strong contrast, and a clear typographic hierarchy.

Across Platforms.

A successful identity should communicate the story before the audience presses play.

10 / Reflection

Designing with Intention.

This project challenged me to think beyond the visual appearance of an identity and consider how design decisions influence the way an audience understands a subject.

By identifying the weaknesses of the original podcast artwork, I was able to focus the redesign on hierarchy, legibility, symbolism, and scalability.

The final direction moves away from ambiguous photographic imagery and toward a more focused graphic system that better reflects the investigative nature of the podcast.

Ultimately, the project reinforced the importance of creating visual identities that are not only engaging, but meaningful, recognizable, and appropriate for the stories they represent.
